# 🎵 About RavenPlayer

RavenPlayer is a powerful offline music player built for Android, combining a beautiful modern interface with advanced audio features and deep customization options.

Designed for music lovers who want more than just playback, RavenPlayer gives users complete control over how they listen, customize, and experience their music.

From professional audio tools to personal music management, RavenPlayer creates a unique listening experience tailored to every user.

---
# ✨ Features

## 🎵 Powerful Music Experience

RavenPlayer provides a complete offline music experience with powerful tools and a beautiful interface.

- 🎧 High-quality offline music playback
- 📂 Smart local music library management
- 🎨 Modern and clean user interface
- ⚡ Fast and smooth performance
- 🔄 Complete control over your music experience
